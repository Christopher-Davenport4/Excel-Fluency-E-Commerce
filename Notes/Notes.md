## Tier 1. Structured tables and formula foundations

So to recap, I learned a few things today. First, I learned that it is good practice to keep the raw CSV file untouched in case something goes wrong. Second, it is good to have a raw sheet within your workbook that is there to duplicate and not edit, for the same reasons. Redundancy is important for taking care of data.

I was also introduced to tables. I have learned about them before, but I had not tried to work with them too much. One of their useful properties is no longer an issue, however, they are still useful because you can directly reference parts of the table, making the syntax cleaner.

Additionally, you can name cells, and they will become absolute references. I also learned that they will forever appear in the dropdown in the top left, making them easy to find. I imagine this would be useful for keeping up with important information you may need on the fly.
